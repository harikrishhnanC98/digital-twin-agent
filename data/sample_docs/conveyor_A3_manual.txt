CONVEYOR SYSTEM A3 — TECHNICAL MANUAL
Version 2.4 | SmartFactory Smart Factory Reference

1. OVERVIEW
Conveyor A3 is a high-speed belt conveyor used in the body-in-white (BIW) assembly line.
It transports car body frames between welding station W7 and the quality inspection gate Q2.
Design throughput: 240 units/hour. Belt width: 1200mm. Drive motor: 7.5 kW SEW Eurodrive.

2. OPERATING PARAMETERS
- Normal belt speed: 0.8 m/s
- Maximum belt speed: 1.2 m/s
- Operating temperature range: 15°C to 40°C
- Motor temperature alarm threshold: 90°C
- Vibration alarm threshold: 3.0 mm/s (RMS)

3. MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE
- Daily: Visual inspection of belt surface and edge guides
- Weekly: Lubrication of drive chain and tensioner bearings
- Monthly: Belt tension check (target: 350 N), roller bearing inspection
- Quarterly: Full drive unit inspection, belt wear measurement (replace at >70% wear)
- Annual: Complete overhaul including motor service and gearbox oil change

4. FAULT CODES
- F01: Belt misalignment detected. Check edge guides. Re-align belt if needed.
- F02: Drive motor overtemperature. Reduce throughput and allow cooling.
- F03: Encoder signal lost. Check encoder cable connection at terminal box TB-A3.
- F04: Emergency stop triggered. Inspect for obstruction before restart.
- F05: Belt slip detected. Check belt tension. Adjust tensioner to 350 N.

5. SAFETY PROCEDURES
Before any maintenance work on Conveyor A3:
- Press the red EMERGENCY STOP button at control panel CP-A3.
- Lock the motor starter at MCC panel MCC-03 using a personal safety lock.
- Hang a "DO NOT OPERATE" tag on the control panel.
- Wait at least 60 seconds for belt to fully stop before entering the guard area.
Failure to follow lockout/tagout (LOTO) procedures is a serious safety violation.

6. DIGITAL TWIN INTEGRATION
Conveyor A3 is fully integrated into the factory digital twin.
Real-time data streams available:
- belt_speed (m/s), updated every 500ms
- motor_temperature (°C), updated every 1s
- vibration_rms (mm/s), updated every 2s
- throughput_count (units/hour), updated every 60s
- belt_wear_percent (%), updated from quarterly inspection logs

The digital twin model ID for Conveyor A3 is: DT-CONV-A3-BIW
Access via the SmartFactory DT API: GET /api/twins/DT-CONV-A3-BIW/telemetry
